# Backlink outreach — how this folder works

A daily automation researches **3–5 hand-verified, topically relevant backlink
opportunities** and writes ready-to-send outreach drafts into `queue/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
Nothing is ever sent automatically — Rudy reviews and sends. `log.json` records every
prospect ever researched so no site is contacted twice.

## Relevance rubric (a prospect must pass ALL of these)

1. **Topical fit** — the site is about consulting, strategy, business development,
   entrepreneurship, executive leadership, or the Montréal / New York business
   ecosystem. If a consulting buyer would never read it, skip it.
2. **Editorial standards** — a human curates what gets published or listed.
   No auto-approve directories, no link farms, no PBNs, no "submit 500 directories" lists.
3. **Authority** — established organization or publication with real traffic and
   its own search visibility (check that the domain itself ranks for something).
4. **Realistic acceptance** — a boutique advisory firm plausibly belongs there.
5. **Link value** — the placement produces a crawlable link or citation
   (directory profile, contributed essay byline, podcast show notes, member listing).

## Priority categories (rotate, best first)

- Curated B2B/consulting directories with review processes (e.g. Clutch, GoodFirms).
- Professional and business associations; Montréal chambers of commerce and
  business boards; NYC business organizations.
- Journalist source-request platforms (Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer,
  Connectively) — answering queries earns citations in real publications.
- Business publications accepting contributed essays on strategy/leadership
  (repurpose Journal essays; always with canonical or unique text).
- Business & strategy podcasts that interview consultants (show notes link).
- Partner ecosystems: dealer/vendor/community pages related to existing partners.
- Local citations: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect
  (NAP must match: RVHL Consulting · info@rvhlconsulting.com · +1 438-395-4624).

## Hard rules

- **Never** pay for links, join link exchanges, or use automated submitters.
- **Never** send outreach automatically — drafts only, a human presses send
  (CASL/CAN-SPAM compliance and brand tone control).
- One quality prospect beats ten mediocre ones; if research yields fewer than
  three genuine passes on a given day, deliver fewer.
